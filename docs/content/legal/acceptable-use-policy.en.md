# DraftURL Acceptable Use Policy

> Effective date: [TBD]
> Last updated: 2026-03-30

> This is a translation provided for your convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Korean version shall prevail.

---

This policy defines prohibited conduct and content in order to maintain a safe and healthy environment for the use of DraftURL (hereinafter the "Service"). This policy forms part of the Terms of Service and applies to all Users.

---

## Article 1 (Prohibited Content)

Users may not post, host, or share any of the following types of content through the Service:

### 1. Illegal Content

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or content that sexually depicts minors
- Content that promotes the trafficking of illegal goods such as drugs or illegal weapons
- Other illegal content prohibited by applicable laws

### 2. Malicious Content

- **Phishing pages**: Pages that impersonate the login pages of other services in order to steal credentials
- **Malware distribution**: Content that contains or distributes malicious software such as viruses, trojans, or ransomware
- **Social engineering**: Content designed to deceive Users into disclosing personal information, financial information, or other sensitive data

### 3. Rights-Infringing Content

- Content that infringes on the intellectual property rights of others, including copyrights, trademarks, and patents
- Content that discloses the personal information of others (real name, address, contact information, photos, etc.) without authorization
- Content that defames or insults others

### 4. Harmful Content

- Content that promotes or glorifies violence, self-harm, or suicide
- Hate speech targeting specific races, genders, religions, nationalities, or other groups
- Content that impersonates or deceives others

### 5. Spam and Abuse

- Automated mass creation of similar documents for SEO manipulation, spam distribution, or similar purposes
- Using the Service for purposes other than its intended use, such as file hosting, mirror sites, or redirect services

---

## Article 2 (Prohibited Conduct)

Users shall not engage in the following conduct:

1. Probing, scanning, or testing the Service's security vulnerabilities (without prior written consent)
2. Intentionally generating excessive load on the Service (DDoS, mass requests, etc.)
3. Attempting to circumvent Service usage restrictions such as rate limiting
4. Reverse engineering, decompiling, or disassembling the Service's source code

---

## Article 3 (Actions Upon Violation)

1. Upon becoming aware of a violation of this policy, the Service may **delete or deactivate** the offending content and restrict Service access for the offending IP.
2. Immediate action without prior warning may be taken in the following cases:
   - Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
   - Malware / phishing pages
   - Legal requests (court orders, investigative authority requests)
   - Attacks on Service infrastructure

---

## Article 4 (Content Reporting)

If you discover content that violates this policy, you may report it through the following method:

- **Email**: abuse@drafturl.com

Please include the following information in your report:

- URL of the offending content
- Type of violation (phishing, copyright infringement, malware, etc.)
- Evidence of the violation (screenshots, etc.)

The Service will complete its review and take action **within 3 business days** of receiving a report.

---

## Article 5 (Copyright Infringement Reporting)

If you discover content that you believe infringes on your copyright, please submit a report including the following information:

1. **Name and contact information** of the copyright owner or authorized representative
2. **Identification of the original work** claimed to have been infringed (original URL, etc.)
3. **URL within the Service** where the infringing content is posted
4. A **statement** that the content is being used without the authorization of the copyright owner

- **Report email**: dmca@drafturl.com

If the report is determined to be legitimate, the offending content will be deactivated or deleted. Service access may be permanently restricted for IPs with repeated infringements.

---

## Article 6 (Cooperation with Law Enforcement)

1. The Service may provide relevant information to law enforcement agencies in response to lawful legal requests (court orders, warrants, etc.).
2. In cases of imminent danger (child safety, threat to life, etc.), relevant information may be provided without a warrant.

---

## Article 7 (Policy Changes)

This policy may be changed in accordance with Service operational circumstances. Significant changes will be announced through the Service website.
